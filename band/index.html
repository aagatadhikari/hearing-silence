    How it works

    Notice the loop. Step out.

    1. 01

      The headband listens for a state

      It looks for the signature of repetitive, self-focused thinking. It reads the state of your attention, never the words.

    2. 02

      The band taps your wrist

      Designed to tap within a second of a loop starting. There’s nothing to read and nothing to answer.

    3. 03

      You come back

      You notice the thought as a thought and return to what you were doing. With practice, the noticing is meant to become yours.

    Where it stands

    A concept, built in the open.

    Hearing Silence is an independent research program. No data has been collected yet. The first phase reanalyses public EEG datasets before anyone wears a thing.

    Every trial will include a sham control. A result that can’t beat sham gets published as a null.

    Questions

    Is it for sale?

    Not yet. The band is a concept from an independent research program. Reserving puts you on the list, and nothing is charged.

    Does it read my thoughts?

    No. It detects a state, whether you’re absorbed in a repetitive loop, never the words. The design rules out decoding content.

    Does it work yet?

    Not yet. No data has been collected. The first phase reanalyses public EEG datasets, and every trial will be sham-controlled.

    Do I need the headband?

    Yes. The headband detects the loop. The band is how you feel it.

    Is it a medical device?

    Not today. It hasn’t been tested, cleared or approved to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
