Anxiety is a loop you can't hear yourself enter.

Most of us only notice we've been replaying a thought long after it started. Hearing Silence is designed to notice for you, quietly, and hand the moment back. No screen, no score, nothing to read.

Two small pieces.
One quiet tap.

It reads the state of your attention, never the content of a thought.

It listens

A tiny in-ear sensor

Rests in your ear and looks for the signature of a worry loop starting. It reads state, never words.

It taps

One gentle tap on your wrist

Designed to answer within about a second. So quiet that only you feel it, with nothing to read and nothing to answer.

You step out

Back to now

Notice the thought as a thought, let it pass, and return to what you were doing. With practice, the noticing is meant to become yours.

The loop begins

The sensor notices the pattern before you do. The band waits, dark and still.

The tap

A single warm light. A single tap.

Never a buzz, never an alarm. Just enough to make you notice where you are.

The quiet after

You step out of the loop.

The light softens. The thought is still there, but you're not inside it anymore.

Is it for sale?
Not yet. The band is a concept from an independent research program. Reserving puts you on the list, and nothing is charged.

      
Does it read my thoughts?
No. It detects a state, whether you're absorbed in a repetitive loop, never the words.

      
Does it work yet?
Not yet. No data has been collected, and every trial will be sham-controlled.

      
Do I need the earpiece?
Yes. The in-ear sensor notices the loop. The band is how you feel it.

      
Is it a medical device?
Not today. It hasn't been tested, cleared or approved to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

      
Are the people in the photos real?
No. The park photos are AI-generated concept images, with the rendered band placed on each wrist.
