# Hearing silence 

Independent research program building EEG neurotechnology that detects anxious thought loops and cues attention back, without reading thoughts.
Hearing Silence

Neurotechnology for anxiety: a quiet tap on the wrist the moment a worry loop begins.

🌐 Live site: https://aagatadhikari.github.io/hearing-silence/

About

Hearing Silence is an independent research program led by Aagat Adhikari. It explores how brain-computer interfaces and real-time EEG can help people notice anxious, repetitive thought sooner and return their attention faster.

The approach decodes state, not content. The device never reads your thoughts. It detects when you have slipped into a self-referential loop and sends a gentle haptic cue within about a second, long before you would have noticed on your own. Over time, the goal is to train the brain's own ability to catch these loops, a skill known as decentering.

The research at a glance
Target: anxious inner speech such as worry, rumination and harsh self-talk.
Method: individually calibrated EEG state decoding paired with moment-by-moment experience sampling.
Deliverables: the Thought-Form Atlas, a first-of-its-kind dataset linking brain recordings to inner experience, and the Attentional Return Index (ARI), a real-time measure of how quickly attention comes back.
